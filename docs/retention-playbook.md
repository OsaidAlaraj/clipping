# Retention playbook (Story Director, 2026-11-24)

Honest evidence grade first: I found **no controlled study** that measures these techniques on short video. What exists is (a) one peer-reviewed line of work on curiosity, (b) creator and vendor guides, (c) anecdotes with numbers and no sample sizes. So every technique below is a **hypothesis to measure on the market corpus** (`docs/market-corpus-for-shots.md`, built by `viral-templates`, not present yet) and with the blind judge, and is adopted only if it wins. Grades: A peer-reviewed, B practitioner consensus (many independent guides agree), C single anecdote.

| # | Technique | Evidence | Grade | When it applies | Render cost | Built |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cold open / teaser**: the strongest later line plays first, then rewind to the start | Cold-open and "start with the payoff" advice converges across guides ([CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/create/short-form-video-hooks), [Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/youtube-shorts-hook-formulas)); anecdote: 2-s retention 62 % to 84 % when starting mid-action with the payoff (unverified Telegram post) | B/C | Clips whose own opening is slow (setup, "so", throat clearing) and that have a quotable stake line in the second half | One extra trim in the existing concat (0 extra encodes) | yes |
| 2 | **Open loop / curiosity gap**: ask or promise early, pay off at the end | Information-gap theory (Loewenstein 1994); [Frontiers in Psychology 2024](https://frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1374649/pdf): incomplete video information raises curiosity and willingness to keep watching. Condition: the gap must be visible, relevant and closable | A (mechanism), B (practice) | Story or explanation moments that really pay off; never a promise the clip does not keep (the hook agent already checks `paid_off`) | none (text) | via hook + plan note |
| 3 | **Contrarian / weird-story openers** | Hook-formula guides, anecdote "how to" to "stop doing" | C | Info and opinion moments | none | hook agent owns text |
| 4 | **Pattern interrupt every 2 to 4 s** (cut, zoom punch, caption emphasis, sound) | Pace claims differ widely: 1 to 4 s ([livecounts on MrBeast](https://livecounts.io/blog/youtube/how-mrbeast-uses-pattern-interrupts-every-4-seconds)), "58 % vs 41 % retention" without a primary source; other guides say a reset every 30 to 60 s | C, conflicting | Static talking-head footage; not gaming or action (already busy) | Zoom punches exist; a denser cadence costs nothing but risks the "breathing picture" the polish bake-off rejected, so only ONE re-hook punch is added | partly |
| 5 | **Re-hook around the middle** | Same guides ("reset", first interrupt late in the clip) | C | Clips over 30 s | One punch + emphasised caption | yes |
| 6 | **End on the peak, no dead outro; loop-friendly end** | Loop guides: end on a line that ties back to the opening ([CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/create/looping-narrative-hooks-watch-time)); rewatch anecdote 4 % to 21 % | B/C | Any clip; drop trailing filler and soft sign-offs | trim only | yes (ending trim) |
| 7 | **Start on the strongest sentence, cut throat-clearing** | Every editor guide; already the engine's tight / hook_first variants | B | Always | none | existing |
| 8 | **Specifics in the first 2 s** (numbers, names, stakes) | Hook-engineering posts; anecdote 3-s retention 68 % with concrete anchors | B/C | Always; used as a score for the cold-open line | none | yes (line scoring) |
| 9 | **Sound design**: riser, hit, silence before a reveal | Vendor guides only | C | Comedy, reveals | needs licensed SFX and a music-policy check | no (policy: natural audio first) |

**Restraint rules (from the owner's standards):** skip the cold open when the clip already opens strongly (a cold open on a good opening is a wasted second); never exceed the campaign's length range; never touch overlays (logo, disclosure label) which are drawn on the final timeline; the teaser must be the creator's own words from the same clip, never invented; at most 4 s long, ends on a sentence end.

**Measure plan.** (1) Blind pairwise judge on the transcript order: original opening vs cold-open opening, both orders. (2) On the market corpus: how often winners use a cold open, their first-2-s specifics, cut pace, and how they end; compare our clips' feature distribution to the winners'. Numbers go to `docs/bakeoff/shots/story-director.md`.

Evidence gaps: no retention-graph data of our own yet; the post-publish views and the owner's decisions (moment_labels) are the first real signal and the learning loop already records them.
