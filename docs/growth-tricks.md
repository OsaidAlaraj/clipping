# Growth tricks: triage of the owner's outside research (2026-11-19)

Source: a long research text the owner brought from another AI (not saved here, citations were stripped). Treated as unverified data. Two high-risk claims were checked against several news reports on 2026-11-19; official policy pages were not opened.

## Verified (multiple independent reports; read the official page before relying on exact wording)
- **YouTube, 1 Oct 2026:** Shorts recommendations reduce reach for re-uploaded clips with nothing added. Not counted as added value: voice-over that only describes the screen, minor technical edits, template-based bulk changes. Counted: own commentary, unique edits, storytelling. No published threshold, and no stated exception for licensed clips.
- **Meta/Facebook, March 2026:** reposts and low-value changes (borders, captions, speed) are treated as unoriginal; reaction-only, watch-along, stitching or narrating what is on screen without new value are deprioritised; duplicates have their distribution reduced; repeat offenders lose monetisation.
- Consequence for ClipMind: **raw clips of permitted campaigns will get less reach on YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels** unless we add real value. TikTok and Instagram rules for this were not checked.

## Unverified claims (do not build on them yet)
Whop figures (12% median effective CPM, 56% of campaigns paid nothing, 10% fee versus 7%, 10-day cycle), TikTok Creator Rewards needing videos over 1 minute, commentary-density thresholds (35 to 45%), Discord server earnings, the "Instagram trial reels" loophole details.

## Adopt
1. **Originality layer.** Record what each clip adds (selection, structure, framing, commentary, visual proof, accessibility) and measure commentary share. Offer an owner-confirmed short intro or outro line (written by the system, in the owner's voice or text on screen). Conflict to settle per campaign: some briefs require raw clips.
2. **Effective-yield ranking.** Use paid-out, approved-submission, views, listed-date, burn rate and runway fields from official or permitted data only, plus the platform fee and cash cycle. Do not scrape sites whose terms forbid it.
3. **Platform packaging.** One master clip becomes per-platform title, caption, hashtags (3 to 5), cover and length.
4. **Retention feedback.** Pull the platforms' own analytics (retention curve, completion, rewatch, shares, saves) for the owner's posts and use them as the learning target.
5. **Hook discipline.** Cold open or redacted pull-forward, keyword said aloud and on screen in the first seconds (TikTok search), loop-friendly endings, sends and saves as the design target for Reels.
6. **Audio.** Voice first; a licensed or royalty-free music bed with ducking; loudness target to be measured (-14 versus -16 LUFS); in-app trending sounds only on the manual path.
7. **Layouts.** 4:5 or 1:1 on a blurred 9:16 canvas, split screen, face-cam stack, safe zones, Arabic RTL templates.
8. **Comment mining and clip families** as content-development tools, not reach tricks.
9. **Arabic.** Dialect-specific hook prompts and a native check by the owner; Najdi, Hijazi, Emirati, Egyptian and Levantine are different.

## Reject (and why)
- **Evading duplicate or integrity detection:** crop, pitch, brightness or noise tweaks meant to defeat perceptual hash or audio fingerprinting, metadata stripping to hide the source, mirror or flip to dodge matching, the "cut 0.3 seconds" trick. It is evasion, it is fragile, and it risks strikes and bans.
- **Device farms, mobile proxies, fingerprint spoofing, VPN warm-up, mass accounts.** Against the owner's rules and the platforms' terms.
- **Replacing a source's audio to dodge a copyright claim.**
- **Manufactured engagement bait** and pinned provocations.
- Honest variation across the owner's own accounts is fine and needed: different cut, hook, commentary and packaging, never the same file.
